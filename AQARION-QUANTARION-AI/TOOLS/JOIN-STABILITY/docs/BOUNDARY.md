# JOIN-STABILITY Boundary

## Included

The primary theorem concerns:

    finite X
    T : X -> X
    equivalence relations E and F
    pullback stability
    lattice join E vee F

## Explicitly excluded

This package does not automatically establish:

- the corresponding infinite theorem
- closure for arbitrary infinite joins
- closure for arbitrary families of equivalence relations
- unrelated lattice operations
- stronger dynamical assumptions
- claims not present in manifest.json

## Infinite case

The infinite-domain question is maintained separately.

A counterexample to an unrestricted infinite statement does not refute the finite theorem.

Likewise, a finite computational census does not establish the infinite statement.

## Evidence

The package distinguishes:

    mathematical proof
    computational verification
    formalization
    reproducibility
    governance

These statuses must not be merged.
