1. ESTABLISHED SURROUNDING MATHEMATICS

Equivalence relations compatible with operations are standard congruences in universal algebra.

For a unary algebra generated by a map T:X\to X, forward-stable equivalence relations are therefore naturally interpreted as congruences.

Quotient construction from a congruence is standard universal algebra.

Therefore AQARION must not claim novelty for:

- the definition of an equivalence relation compatible with T;
- existence of quotient dynamics for a compatible equivalence;
- generic congruence closure under join;
- the ordinary congruence-lattice viewpoint.

[
\boxed{

The exact theorem and proof architecture require dedicated literature comparison.

EXACT MATCH
CLOSE ANALOGUE
GENERAL FRAMEWORK
RELATED BUT DIFFERENT
NOT RELEVANT

For every exact or close match record:

- theorem statement;
- hypotheses;
- conclusion;
- notation translation;
- proof method;
- publication date;
- whether finite cardinality is used;
