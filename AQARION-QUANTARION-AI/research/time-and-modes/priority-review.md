## Mathematical equivalence check

For positive radii, let r = R_front/R_rear.

Then:

    Q = (r - 1)/(r + 1)
    r = (1 + Q)/(1 - Q)

Thus Q and instantaneous radius ratio contain equivalent information.
A new normalization alone is not a novelty claim.
