What this demonstrates

A mechanical oscillator and a series RLC circuit can be written as the same idealized second-order linear equation after choosing the corresponding parameters and variables.

MECHANICAL ELECTRICAL ------------------------------------------------ m x'' + b x' + kx = F L q'' + R q' + q/C = V m <-> L b <-> R k <-> 1/C x <-> q x' <-> i F0 <-> V0 ------------------------------------------------

This is a mathematical analogy between idealized models, not evidence that a real mechanical oscillator and a real electrical circuit are the same physical system.
