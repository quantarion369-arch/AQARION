# Temporal manuscript archive: publication scope

## Version identities
The original v0.1 manuscript and its existing review packet are historical
version-specific records. The v0.1.1 packet is the preserved working revision.
Publishing the older review packet does not represent review of v0.1.1.

The complete v0.1 bundle is byte-identical to both preserved copies:
the review packet's paper-bundle and the revision's evidence/parent-v01.
Manuscript SHA-256 identities are recorded in the publication review JSON.

## What preparation verifies
Existing packet files and local modes are fingerprinted. All discovered
SHA256SUMS manifests are checked locally. Top-level preparation receipts
are zero. These receipts concern packet preparation, not independent
proof review or fresh execution of embedded computational evidence.

## Boundaries
No manuscript, attribution, historical status, provenance, source snapshot,
or historical manifest is edited. This preparation does not execute packet
scripts, compilers, evidence programs, or archived binaries.
It does not perform a new manuscript-diff review, full source audit,
independent theorem review, formal verification, or literature comparison.
Earlier assistant review must not be represented as independent peer review.

This is archival repository publication, not journal submission, reviewer
contact, acceptance, novelty certification, or application-claim promotion.
Outstanding scholarly review and priority questions are not closed here.

## Custody and scope
All files in the three named packets are included as preserved records,
including embedded evidence and historical duplicates.
Other papers, the registry backup, caches, and unrelated files are excluded.
The review JSON records original local POSIX modes; Git does not preserve
every local permission bit.
The publication manifest covers all originals and two publication documents;
it excludes its own checksum. Preparation precedes any later commit or push.
