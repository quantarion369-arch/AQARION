# Elementary terminal-repair lemma

Let the prefix range over the full d-cube. Proper prefix monomials span a codimension-one space W, missing only the full product.

Expand a Boolean terminal f in its unique real/rational multilinear polynomial. If f is non-affine, choose a nonzero coefficient alpha_S of maximum degree >=2. The permitted temporal product f times all prefix coordinates outside S uses at most d-1 observables. Its top coefficient is alpha_S, since all strict supersets have zero coefficients.

Its parity moment equals (-1)^d alpha_S and is nonzero. Together with W this yields full rank 2^d at degree d-1.

An affine Boolean function over the reals is constant or a coordinate literal/complement. Such terminals do not introduce the missing direction.

This lemma assumes a bijective full-cube prefix. It does not replace the external spectral reduction for arbitrary separating systems.
