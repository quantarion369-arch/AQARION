# Polynomial certificate evidence chain

This package preserves three completed local finite audits:

1. Prime-power baseline.
2. Reciprocal-polynomial and second-kind modular audit.
3. Exact-residue, lifted-modulus, and negative-control addendum.

The polynomial audit concerns reciprocal elementary symmetric
sums for six positive polynomial sequences, n=1,...,20,
and primes 2,3,5,7,11.

Its nonintegrality flags concern those reciprocal sums.
They are not canonical cycle-ratio certificates without
the additional factorial and partition normalization.

The second-kind audit covers n=1,...,40 and
1<=k<=min(n,12), for the same five primes.
Periodicity concerns factorial(k)*S(n,k), not minimum
periods of the unscaled second-kind sequence.

The semantic addendum checks exact leading residues,
414 exact inclusion-exclusion identities, 2070 lifted-modulus
recoveries, 6190 recurrence-based period comparisons,
and rejection of twelve deliberately corrupted controls.

Historical executed sources are preserved unchanged.
They retain their original local dependency paths.
This is not a claim of standalone portable replay.

No experiments were rerun during packaging.
No new infinite proof or formal certification is supplied.
Novelty clearance and global classification remain OPEN.
Promotion remains false.
