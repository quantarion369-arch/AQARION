COMPUTATIONAL SCOPE AND MATHEMATICAL DEPENDENCIES

Object: K[S], with the ten semigroup generators in MODEL.json.
No archived differential matrix is used in this computation.
Archived semigroup shifts are compared only AFTER homology is computed.

1. S is the cone lattice points:
   a_i>=0 and a_i+a_j-k>=0.
   The constructive induction from the structural proof establishes
   equality with the monoid generated by the ten displayed vectors.

2. A unimodular generator submatrix spans Z^5.
   The ten primitive facets have value one at kappa=(1,1,1,1,1).
   Interior lattice points equal kappa+S.
   Standard normal-semigroup results give Cohen-Macaulayness and
   a principal canonical module over every field.
   Codimension is five.

3. The multigraded top shift is
   c=sum(g_j)-kappa=(4,4,4,4,4).
   Self-duality of the abstract minimal resolution pairs beta(i,b)
   with beta(5-i,c-b).
   A nonzero Betti degree belongs to S; so does its dual degree.
   Hence every possible Betti degree lies in S intersect (c-S).

4. This interval is exactly:
   0<=a_i<=4, and
   max_pair(a_i+a_j)-4 <= k <= min_pair(a_i+a_j).
   Its 835-degree enumeration is a justified completeness bound,
   conditional on the preceding structural statements.

5. The divisor complex contains F exactly when
   b-sum(g_j:j in F) belongs to S.
   The augmented boundary uses vertices 0..9 in increasing order.
   beta(i,b)=dim H_tilde(i-1,Delta_b;K)
   by the divisor-complex/Koszul formula.
   Negative k is harmless: the rank-five facet map embeds S in N^10.

6. Integral reduced homology has free rank
   cells(d)-rank(boundary_d)-rank(boundary_(d+1)).
   Its torsion factors are the nonunit Smith factors of
   boundary_(d+1).
   Indeed, coker(boundary_(d+1)) maps onto the free group
   im(boundary_d), with kernel H_d; the torsion groups agree.

7. Every boundary is also reduced to a unit diagonal plus zero
   using integer unit pivots.
   The saved pivot trace is replayed against the original boundary.
   Unit-pivot elimination corresponds to unimodular row/column
   operations and independently certifies absence of torsion.

8. Torsion-free integral reduced homology in every candidate degree
   implies field-independent Betti dimensions by universal
   coefficients.
   Completeness outside this interval uses steps 1-4 over every field.
