# Graph-quotient experiment archive: publication scope

This record covers five preserved local experiment packages:
quotient-01, boundary-01, one-sided-01, extension-01-MlFtzHx7,
and families-01-GVWU4n3Z.

## Evidence roles

The packages preserve:
- a weighted quotient/walk-count example;
- closure, commutation, and hidden-reconstruction boundary examples;
- a closure-without-commutation example;
- delayed-reconstruction and positive reversible Markov examples;
- bounded delay and rank-gap families.

These are sources and historical execution records. This preparation
does not rerun them or independently authenticate their historical execution.

The first three packages have historical source-only checksum records.
Their package-prefixed paths are resolved against the enclosing graph
experiment directory. Original records are not rewritten.

The extension and family packages also have historical package manifests.

The publication checksum file binds the current copies of all 27 original
files plus these publication documents. That does not retroactively turn
source-only historical records into historical full-package certificates.

## Limits

No general formal proof, novelty clearance, physical interpretation,
PB certification, or application capability is established by this archive.
Bounded family checks retain their original finite ranges.

Output logs are intentional evidence, including where Git ignores them.
They must not be silently omitted from the reviewed publication batch.

All material remains under the existing graph research subtree.
No repository-root research file is created.
