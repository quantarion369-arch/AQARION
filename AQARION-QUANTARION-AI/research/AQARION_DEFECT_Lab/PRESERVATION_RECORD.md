## Reported execution evidence

relative to its paired baseline at horizons 1, 2, 4, and 8 in all
ten pairs. These are repeated evaluations of ten model pairs.

hashes, numerical geometry bounds, and ridge-component decomposition
were reported as passed.

Reconstruction benefit is inconsistent. Generalization to other
dynamical systems and independent reproduction remain unestablished.

The successful seed-369 three-arm Termux feasibility run is distinct
from the later optimizer-initialization failure. Do not merge them.
