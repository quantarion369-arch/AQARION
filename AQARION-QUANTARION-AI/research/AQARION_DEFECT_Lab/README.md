# AQARION_DEFECT_Lab

Experimental research preservation snapshot.

## Scope

- Colab: width-three paired replication, saved models, and supplied audits.
- Termux: saved development files and checkpoints.
- Original archives are retained alongside extracted readable copies.
- Downloaded notebook snapshots are included when available.

## Evidence boundary

User-supplied execution receipts report ten paired prediction improvements
at horizons 1, 2, 4, and 8 on the same noisy eight-state cycle.
Reconstruction benefit is inconsistent.
This import does not independently reproduce training or establish
generalization to other systems.

The published Hugging Face model is not replaced by these artifacts.
QR/unridged labels refer to floating-point projector geometry,
not symbolic or rational certification.

## Preservation limitations

Runtime-history source snapshots are not a complete notebook.
Unsaved Termux stdin sessions may not be recoverable.
Earlier Colab results outside the replication archive may still be missing.
Historical failure records are retained where present.
Review all contents before public publication.
